Our Mission

Digital infrastructure for neighborhood commerce.

To build the digital commerce infrastructure that enables India’s neighborhood retailers to participate in the digital economy. Mazishop connects local stores, their inventory and nearby customers through technology — helping retailers become discoverable, accessible and digitally enabled without requiring dark stores or large physical infrastructure.

Our Story

From a local retail problem to a scalable commerce vision.

Mazishop began in April 2025 with a shared vision to solve a growing gap in India’s neighborhood retail ecosystem.

The idea was initiated by Yash Khadgi, who identified how local retailers were losing digital visibility while customers were increasingly moving toward online discovery, convenience and home delivery.

Yash then brought together Rameshwar Parmar, Mohit Dakhode and Aryan Fulzele. The four founders came together with a common vision: to digitally empower existing neighborhood retailers instead of replacing them.

The Problem

A massive retail network, with a digital gap.

Neighborhood retailers already have stores, inventory and local relationships. What many lack is a simple digital layer that connects those strengths with modern customer expectations.

01

Limited Digital Visibility

Customers often cannot easily discover what nearby stores have available.

02

Technology Gap

Many local retailers do not have integrated tools for digital catalogues, inventory and orders.

03

Changing Expectations

Consumers increasingly expect online discovery, price visibility and convenient local delivery.

What We Are Building

Digitizing existing stores — not replacing them.

Mazishop is building a technology layer around neighborhood retail. We are not building a new dark-store network; we are enabling existing stores to participate in digital commerce.
